Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

The short version

Unbound does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reports, no cookies, and no tracking of any kind.

Data we collect

None. Unbound runs entirely on your machine. We have no servers that receive data from the app, and no telemetry of any kind is built into the software.

AI features

Unbound includes optional AI-powered features: auto-captions, translation, AI chat, and voice dubbing. When you choose to use these features, data is sent to Huru (huruai.xyz) and/or the 0G Compute Network for processing. Specifically:

  • Auto-captions and translation send audio or text to Huru's API for transcription and translation.
  • AI chat sends your messages to Huru's language model API.
  • Voice dubbing sends audio to Huru for speech synthesis.

These features are entirely opt-in. If you don't use them, no data leaves your machine. When you do use them, data is sent only to the providers listed above and is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Local processing

The following features are processed entirely on your device with no network requests:

  • Screen recording and video editing
  • Voice cloning (voice profile extraction)
  • Lip sync (Wav2Lip)
  • Cursor effects, auto-zoom, backgrounds, and export

Accounts and authentication

Unbound does not require an account. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profiles. If you connect a wallet for 0G Compute features, that interaction is between you and the 0G network — Unbound does not store your wallet address or keys.

Cookies and tracking

Unbound uses zero cookies. This website does not use analytics, tracking pixels, or any third-party scripts. There are no ads.

Open source

Unbound is open source under the AGPLv3 license. You can audit the source code to verify every claim on this page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected in the date at the top of this page and in the project's Git history.
